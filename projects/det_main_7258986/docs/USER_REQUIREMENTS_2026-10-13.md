The game should feel like a skirmish that slowly ramps up into a major
engagement over the course of the match. Players in smaller ships should have
their skills with the flight mechanics greatly rewarded, with plenty of
opportunity to showcase their skill like in counterstrike. Players in larger
ships should be rewarded for positioning skills, stepping out of position
should be rewarded quickly with death, while excellent positioning should see
them rack up kills with flak and point defense while slowly pushing across the
map to a point where they can threaten the enemy mothership. That should be
the goal of the game, pushing lanes to destroy the enemy mothership; like a
MOBA combined with Counter Strike, combined with Natural Selection 2's
commander seat (swapped for an "admiral" here). The match should be 13 vs 13,
with 12 players in the field and 1 in the admiral chair on each team. Like a
MOBA, there should be 3 distinct lanes, joined together by a lane running
perpendicular to the others down the middle, running from 1 mothership to the
next, with "minions", which are low skill bots, that spawn from the mothership
regularly and travel down each lane. The minions should come in a wave of 10,
with 5 fighters, 4 bombers, and 1 escort corvette, the corvette being like the
cannon minion in League of Legends, somewhat larger with more health, and
longer range weapons. Additionally, between the lanes should be a "jungle",
think league of legends or DOTA, which is filled with asteroids and that only
small ships can travel through, and is populated throughout by "monsters",
derelict ships with automated turrets, and sometimes automated snub fighters,
which can be destroyed for a personal reward. Larger ships should not be
inherently better than smaller ships, both should be needed to push lanes or
prevent lane pushes.

Players should also have their own items they can purchase separate from the
admiral, which upgrades their pilot with stat boost items. The stat boost
should affect things like ship maneuverability, ship speed, acceleration,
increase income generation over time, total ship energy pool available, weapon
firing speed, shield regeneration, shield strength, hull points, hull damage
resistance, shield damage resistance, and other stats you think should be
important. The players 6 item slots. A flight suit, helmet, stick, cockpit
layout, chair, and implant. There should be several choices for each slot that
emphasize different stats, obviously certain stats are more important for
certain types of ships.

Ships should have shields which regenerate over time if not attacked. This
should be an additional health pool that increases time to kill overall in the
game from its current state. The shield should have 4 zones, front, back and
sides, that the pilot can redirect "shield power" between with a cooldown that
scales up with ship size. Pilots should be able to redirect "energy" between 3
ship systems, weapons, shields, and thrusters, with a cooldown that scales up
with ship size.

Resource nodes should not be able to be mined by players at hardpoints, do away
with that mechanic. Repair bays should not be be built at resource nodes but
instead be spawned by admirals can be flown about the map. Repair bays should
be small enough to fly into the "jungle", but stop repairing when damage.
Repair bays should have an admiral upgrade to equip them with point defense and
shields, when the shields only are damaged the bay should continue repair.
Repair bays repair things by sending out "repair drones" to nearby ships.
Repair bays start with only 1 drone but can be upgraded to carry 4. Larger
ships can be repaired by more than 1 drone at a time, and the largest ships can
be repaired by 4 at a time, but repair speed should be relatively slow for
large ships. Ships can be repaired while under fire, but the drones repairing
them can be destroyed to stop repairs. Repair bays cannot move once a location
has been chosen for them. Do away with resource extractors and automatic
extractors, we have already replaced them with mining drones.

The admiral should once again be able to build turrets at resource nodes. The
resource nodes in the middle of the map can be populated only with point
defense turrets while those closer to the mothership can be populated with
torpedo turrets that can damage larger ships more effectively. These turrets
should be hard to destroy by players on their own, but much easier with minions
around as the turrets get distracted by shooting the minions. The player should
want to push their minion waves up to the turrets to damage them and let the
player get free shots in on the turrets. If a player shoots a player from
another team near an enemy turret, they should become a priority for turret
fire. Minions should only do a small amount of damage to turrets but it will
add up overtime.

Add asteroid collision damage but it should be relatively forgiving. Very
large ships can use their guns to destroy asteroids, but not resource nodes,
with concentrated fire, allowing them to destroy the "jungle" later in the game
if they want to open up more or wider lanes.

The admiral should be able to unlock a late game module that allows them to
directly control minions, but this limits the number of friendly minions
available on the map at any time to 30 and makes them cost resources to
replace instead of spawning for free every set amount of time.

Remove the dynamic event zones.

Make it so players cannot see through asteroids.

Allow the admiral to place "wards" on the map anywhere a friendly player
currently has vision. These "wards" are a drone that flies from the mothership
to the selected location and provides vision in a small area around it. They
can quickly be destroyed if attacked. The wards cannot move once a location has
been chosen for them

Remove pilot independent extractors

Remove pilot independent node buff markers.

Make it so pilots have to buy personal repair kits at the mothership, where
they take up an item slot until they are used or discarded intentionally. Think
how health potions work on league of legends. The drone the repair kits deploy,
however, can be destroyed as they are the same repair drones the repair bays
use, just weaker at healing and short in duration.

lock players to a max zoom that is close to their ship which scales with ship
size. Not unreasonably close though, just close enough that they cant see too
much of what goes on outside their lane, but they do have a minimap. Admirals
can look anywhere, even when flaying a ship outside the mothership.

only destroying the mothership should end the match

The mothership should no longer become weaker after large treasury spends, but
it should become weaker if many players, minions not included, are respawning
quickly. The mothership should have a lot of health and decent point defenses,
but it should be weak to attacks by large ships. There should be upgrades the
admiral can unlock to let the mothership in theory become the most powerful
ship on the field by far if everything was unlocked, able to fight multiple
frigates at once and (barely) win. The mothership should be able to be moved
by a docked admiral, that being an admiral who is not outside the mothership in
their own ship, starting at 20 minutes.

A player should be able to choose to be admiral at the start of the match, and
if they refuse they will be locked into not being the admiral for the rest of
the match. The admiral will instead be an AI player. Only 1 admiral allowed per
team, obviously.
