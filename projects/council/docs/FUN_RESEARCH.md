# Fun research (raw output of the research workflow, 2026-10-15)

Three researchers (player motivation, competitive design benchmarks, an
audit of this game) fed a lead designer, whose plan was then checked by a
critic. This file is that plan, unedited apart from the title. The rules
for USING it are in docs/FUN_PLAN.md, which overrides this file where they
differ (in particular: the user's 2026-10-15 directive that ideas which
break a requirement are NOT rejected, only flagged).


## Fun model

### [12] 1. Decisive match arc: match length and a guaranteed ending
- Why: Only a mothership kill ends a match, with no surrender, so the design itself must close games. In the baseline, 3 of 6 seeds (11-16) hit the 60:00 cap, and decided stomps dragged on: final_5 led 42-15 on kills at 15:00 and ended at 46:13. Reference points: LoL about 30 min and Dota about 40 min (public stats), Allegiance 30-45 min with painful 2-hour tails. The slow ramp the user asked for (frigates at 10-12 min, capitals at 16-25, a movable mothership from 20:00) needs roughly 30-40 min.
- Metric: Existing FUN_JSON fields `ended` and `minutes`. Per seed, m = minutes if the match ended, otherwise 60. Report the median, IQR, p90 and ended_rate over the batch, counting unended seeds as 60.
- Target: Median 30-40 min; 90% of seeds within 22-55 min; ended_rate >= 95%; at most 5% of matches under 20 min.

### [10] 2. Endgame arc: a visible push, a close the defender can answer, no limbo, won by big ships
- Why: Audit pain point 1. Motherships go untouched until a median of about 41 min. The loser then dies a median 65 s after its last full-hull sample, or a stalled siege leaves a crippled mothership hanging for 7-23 min. The requirement says the mothership has 'a lot of health' and is 'weak to attacks by large ships', so endings should come from big-ship pushes, not minions or timers.
- Metric: Probe v2 samples each mothership's hull fraction every 5 s and TeamState.siege_phase every 1 s. (a) ms_pressure_onset_min: the first time either mothership's hull fraction drops below 0.95 (-1 = never). (b) final_push_secs: match end minus the last time the LOSING mothership was at >= 0.75 hull (ended seeds only). (c) ms_limbo_secs: the longest stretch in which a mothership is at <= 0.70 hull AND lost less than 0.02 hull fraction over the preceding 120 s (max of the two motherships). (d) siege_bite_rate: over assault phases lasting >= 20 s (both teams, pooled over seeds), the share in which the target's hull fell by >= 0.10 between assault start and siege end; this needs the waypoint bug fixed in main first. (e) ms_big_damage_share: the share of the losing mothership's hull damage (from the new Events.damage_applied hook, hull_dmg > 0) dealt by LARGE or CAPITAL pilot ships, pooled over ended seeds.
- Target: Onset 22-34 min; final push 120-420 s; limbo <= 180 s; bite rate 35-70%; big-ship damage share >= 0.60.

### [13] 3. Suspense and earned comebacks
- Why: Close games are more enjoyable even for the winner (Abuhamdeh et al. 2015), and suspense keeps people watching (Simonov et al., CS:GO viewership). The worth-based lead in the current probe is broken: about 70% of pilot income is shared, so worth never diverges by more than 1.2x and the probe reports 0 comebacks. Yet by kills, 3 of 9 finished matches were won from behind at 15:00, while stomps still took 30+ min to end. The target bands are design judgement, not published Riot figures.
- Metric: A new adv_index, sampled every 30 s, lives in tests/lib/adv_index.gd. It is measurement-only: no game script may import it. It ignores credits: A = (TA_H - TA_C)/3 + (K5_H - K5_C)/6 + (FV_H - FV_C)/4000 + 6*(MS_H - MS_C). TA = the team's alive turrets. K5 = enemy pilot ships the team's pilots killed in the last 300 s. FV = the sum of bounty_value() over the team's alive pilot ships. MS = mothership hull fraction. The lead L = +1 if A >= 1, -1 if A <= -1, otherwise 0 (Halcyon's view). (a) adv_lead_changes: times L flips to the opposite non-zero sign and holds for 2 samples. (b) comeback15: the winner's L at 15:00 was -1; a rate over ended seeds. (c) even15_share: the share of seeds with L = 0 at 15:00. (d) decided_to_end_min: end time minus the start of the winner's final unbroken L = +1 run (for unended seeds, 60 minus the start of the final leader run). The weights and threshold are pre-registered. After baseline v2, report the AUC of sign(A) predicting the winner at 15, 20 and 25 min. Only if AUC at 20 min is below 0.65, switch once to the alternative (drop FV, double K5), then freeze it. Keep the old worth fields for continuity.
- Target: Median lead changes >= 2 (at least 1 in 70% of seeds); comeback15 20-35%; even15 30-50%; median decided_to_end <= 8 min.

### [12] 4. Low downtime (dead + commute + idle)
- Why: Time not playing is the quiet killer. On this map, the 'travel' bucket (0.38 of pilot time) is about as large as time spent fighting. Dead share climbs from 3.5% early to 19-25% after 40 min, and a late capital death costs 42-50 s of respawn plus a 2:45-4:50 flight back. Allegiance players' main complaint was the long flight back after death. The brief still wants Homeworld-scale trips (20-60+ s), so travel should shrink, not vanish.
- Metric: (a) travel: the existing 1-s bucket (alive, not engaged, no enemy within 2,500), reported as commute (speed >= 0.4 x f_max) plus idle, and scored on the total. (b) time_share.dead (existing). (c) dead_late: dead + spooling pilot-seconds divided by all pilot-seconds after 30:00. (d) rta_late_s: the median, over pilot deaths after 30:00, of the time from death to the first later sample in which that pilot is engaged. Report how many deaths were dropped for never re-engaging, and also report rta_all_s and the docked share (the AI shops instantly; human hangar time belongs to ingredient 12).
- Target: Travel <= 0.30 (report commute and idle); dead 0.05-0.13; dead_late <= 0.15; rta_late <= 150 s (rta_all 45-100 s).

### [11] 5. A skirmish that ramps into a major engagement
- Why: This is the first sentence of the user's requirements. The Left 4 Dead AI Director and pacing research both argue that peaks need build-up and lulls. In the baseline, team fights peak at 8-14 of 24 pilots, happen 3.5-4.4 times per 10 min and fill 9-15% of match time.
- Metric: Probe v2 builds single-linkage clusters of alive pilot ships every 2 s (link distance <= 2,500). A cluster is a fight if it holds >= 3 ships of each team AND, within the last 4 s, a member took a hit from a pilot of the other team (from the damage hook). Its size is its ship count. Keep the largest fight per sample, binned early (< 10:00), mid (10-25) and late (>= 25:00). (a) fight_p90_late: the 90th percentile of the largest fight size over late fight samples. (b) fight_ramp: mean largest late fight / mean largest early fight. (c) time_share.engaged (existing). (d) pk_ramp: pilot-caused pilot deaths per pilot-minute after 25:00, divided by the same before 10:00.
- Target: fight_p90_late >= 14 (a fight of 16+ pilots after 25:00 in >= 70% of seeds); fight_ramp 1.6-3.0; engaged 0.40-0.60; pk_ramp 1.3-3.0.

### [10] 6. Pilots, not bots, decide fights (lose only to what you can fight)
- Why: Deaths to scenery or bots feel illegitimate; deaths to an opponent who outplayed you are accepted (the counterplay principle). DESIGN says pilots should die mostly to pilots. Instead, minions kill 16% of pilots before 10:00 and 42-43% after 30:00, and late turrets fall to minion waves with no pilot near.
- Metric: (a) pilot_kill_share (existing). (b) pilot_kill_share_late: the same over deaths after 30:00. (c) pilot_involved_death_share: the share of pilot-ship deaths where Economy.contributors(victim, 15 s, 0.10), read at Events.unit_killed (Combat.kill emits it before remove_unit, so the damage_log is intact), contains an enemy pilot. (d) unattended_turret_loss_share: the share of Events.turret_destroyed events with no alive pilot ship of the destroying team within 3,000 of the turret.
- Target: pilot_kill_share >= 0.65 (minions <= 0.20, turrets + mothership <= 0.15, derelicts <= 0.08, rocks <= 0.03); late >= 0.55; involved >= 0.80; unattended <= 0.10.

### [9] 7. Skill expression and readable time-to-kill in small ships
- Why: The requirement says small-ship flight skill should be 'greatly rewarded ... like Counter-Strike'. In self-determination research, competence predicts enjoyment, but results that are fully predetermined kill suspense. Kills must last long enough for the victim to react (shield focus, routing, escape), and the requirement says shields should increase TTK.
- Metric: (a) skill_rho_small: the Spearman correlation, across all AI pilots in a match, between PilotAI.skill and their small-hull score (kills + 0.5 x assists)/(deaths + 1), counting only events while the pilot flies a SMALL hull. Report the median over seeds. Skill jitter (--skill-jitter: seat skill = 0.5 + 0.45 x rand, the same for both teams, from a separate RNG seeded by --seed) is on in every FUN batch. Rule: AI use of a new mechanic may not add a new skill threshold. (b) duel_skill_winrate: tests/arena.gd with new --skill-a/--skill-b flags, 20 fighter 1v1 duels, skill 0.90 vs 0.55, sides alternated; computed once per build. (c) ttk_small_med_s: per ship, a damage chain starts at a hit (from the damage hook) that comes more than 4 s after the previous hit; at death, ttk = time minus chain start. Report the median over SMALL-hull deaths. (d) burst_death_share_small: the share of SMALL-hull deaths with ttk < 1.5 s.
- Target: rho 0.35-0.65; duel win rate 0.70-0.85; small-ship TTK median 4-10 s as a provisional design band, reset after baseline v2, and never below the baseline median; burst deaths <= 5%.

### [8] 8. Both sizes needed; big ships live and die by position
- Why: The requirements say larger ships should not be inherently better, that both sizes should be needed, and that big-ship pilots who step out of position should die quickly, while good positioning lets them 'rack up kills with flak and point defense'. Per-hull kill rates in the baseline are confounded by seat skill and role: the junglers fly Lances and the weakest seats fly Fighters.
- Metric: (a) size_parity = |log2(I_small / I_big)|, where I = (pilot kills + 0.5 x assists + 1.5 x turret participations + 0.05 x minion last hits + 0.1 x derelict kills) per pilot-minute, for SMALL vs LARGE+CAPITAL hulls (medium excluded). Turret participation = credits_earned reason 'turret'. Pooled over the jittered batch. (b) arena_small_winrate: the mean win rate of the small side over 4 equal-credit arena matchups, 10 runs each, all pilots at skill 0.72, sides alternated: 7 fighters vs 1 flak frigate; 3 bombers vs 1 flak frigate; 6 bombers vs 1 destroyer; 4 lances vs 1 ion frigate. Computed once per build. (c) big_flak_pd_kill_share: the share of pilot kills by LARGE/CAPITAL pilots whose killing blow is a flak or PD weapon. (d) big_pos_gap: the median TTK (from ingredient 7's chains) of isolated LARGE/CAPITAL deaths divided by that of supported ones. Isolated = no friendly pilot ship and fewer than 3 friendly minions within 3,000 at chain start. (e) small_late_kill_share: the share of pilot kills after 25:00 made by SMALL hulls.
- Target: Size parity <= 0.58 (within 1.5x); arena small-side mean 0.40-0.60; flak/PD kill share 0.25-0.60; big_pos_gap <= 0.5; small late kill share 0.30-0.60.

### [6] 9. An economy that reflects play and always has something to buy
- Why: Feedback on progress and meaningful choices are near-universal player needs. In the baseline, about 70% of pilot income is shared, pilots hold 2.7k unspent credits at 30:00, treasuries reach 17-89k late, and the AI tech plan finishes at 31-35 min. PROGRESS left 'whether to add a repeatable sink' as the user's decision, so sinks that win must be flagged to the user at merge.
- Metric: (a) play_income_share: pilot credits from Events.credits_earned with reasons {kill, assist, minion, turret, derelict, repair bay, drone, ward, repair drone, plus any new bounty reasons}, divided by the sum of PilotProfile.earned. (b) earn_spread: the median over seeds of p75/p25 of the AI pilots' earned. (c) pilot_float_30 (existing money_30). (d) treasury_float_late: the mean treasury of both teams, sampled every 30 s after 30:00. Also report corr(skill, earned).
- Target: Play income share 0.45-0.70; earn spread 1.3-2.0; pilot float at 30:00 <= 1,200; mean late treasury <= 3,000.

### [5] 10. The admiral seat matters all match without being a single point of failure
- Why: Allegiance, NS2 and Battlefield 2 show that commanding is fun when decisions are frequent, legible and impactful. The bad outcomes are a seat that alone decides matches and a seat that goes idle. The original brief says the team must not collapse under a weak admiral. In the baseline, the AI plan completes by 31-35 min, then money piles up.
- Metric: (a) late_spend_ratio: treasury spent after 30:00 divided by treasury income after 30:00 (income = change in treasury + change in TeamState.spent). (b) admiral_decisions_per_min_late: Events.admiral_result events with ok == true, per team-minute after 30:00, counting treasury-spending actions except 'drone' plus mothership moves, capped at one per action type per 30 s. (c) asset_kill_share: the share of pilot kills where the victim died within 2,000 of a killer-team turret, within 1,400 of a killer-team bay or ward, or inside the killer team's active waypoint ring. The A/B test against a weak admiral is a gate, not part of the score.
- Target: Late spend ratio >= 0.75; 0.5-3 decisions per minute; asset kill share 0.30-0.70.

### [4] 11. Teamwork and AI teammates you would want
- Why: Relatedness predicts enjoyment (Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski 2006). With one human and 25 bots, the social frustration comes from AI allies who feed or sit out fights. Two junglers per team farm all match, and the jungle share stays flat at 15-16% of pilot samples through 60:00.
- Metric: (a) assisted_kill_share: the share of pilot kills with >= 2 distinct enemy-pilot contributors (>= 10% of damage in the last 15 s). (b) low_kp_pilots: per team, pilots with (kills + assists)/(team pilot kills) < 0.08; the mean of both teams. (c) late_presence: after 25:00, the share of (pilot x 5-min window) pairs in which the pilot was in at least one fight cluster (ingredient 5's definition).
- Target: Assisted share 0.50-0.80; at most 1 low-participation pilot per team; late_presence >= 0.75.

### [0] 12. Not measurable headless: flight feel, duel readability and human downtime
- Why: This is the heart of 'like Counter-Strike': controls, hit feedback, and shield-zone and routing cues at the locked zoom. Human hangar and shopping time is invisible to the probe because the AI shops instantly.
- Metric: Not measurable headless. Judge it in human playtests after each merged batch. (1) Controls check: A/D strafe and turn as labelled under the chase camera, and a new pilot hits a moving fighter within 60 s. (2) A short PENS questionnaire (competence, autonomy, intuitive controls) and GEQ tension/frustration items after each match. (3) 'Why did you die?' answers scored against the logged death cause. (4) Hangar seconds per death for the human pilot. (5) Screenshot and video review (tests/spectate_shots.gd) of shield-zone hits and any new routing, overcharge or slipstream cues at small- and capital-ship zoom.
- Target: PENS items at or above the scale midpoint + 1; frustration at or below the midpoint; deaths correctly understood >= 80%; human hangar time <= 20 s per death; every merged idea that adds a cue passes the screenshot review.

### [0] 13. Not measurable headless: legibility and admiral cognitive load
- Why: Strategy interest is falling and players hit cognitive overload sooner (Quantic Foundry), and MOBAs already score high on frustration. Every new mechanic (bounties, breach, slipstream, admiral assets) must be announced to both teams or it reads as random.
- Metric: Not measurable headless. (1) Forks ship a feed line and a HUD tag per mechanic; before merge, each accepted mechanic also gets a minimap icon and a help-panel line, verified with tests/ui_tour.gd screenshots. (2) Timed human admiral tasks ('sweep the top jungle', 'post a bounty on the bot inner turret'), each 10 s or less. (3) A fork adds at most 4 admiral buttons. (4) Post-session question: 'would you play another match?'
- Target: All checklist items present at merge; each admiral task <= 10 s; at least 70% answer yes to 'would you play another match?'.

## Fun score formula
CURVES: T(x; a,b,c,d) is a trapezoid: 0 at or below a, rising linearly to 100 at b, 100 from b to c, falling to 0 at d, and 0 at or above d. 'Lower is better' curves read '100 at <= c, 0 at >= d'; 'higher is better' curves read '0 at <= a, 100 at >= b'. Per-seed metrics are scored per seed, then averaged. Pooled rates and ratios (siege_bite_rate, ms_big_damage_share, comeback15, even15, size_parity, skill_rho_small, big_pos_gap, big_flak_pd_kill_share, small_late_kill_share, assisted share) are scored once on the pooled value. Arena metrics (duel_skill_winrate, arena_small_winrate) are computed once per build, which takes about 0.5 h. INGREDIENT SCORES: S1 (w12) = mean over seeds of T(minutes; 18, 28, 42, 58), unended = 0. S2 (w10) = mean of onset T(15, 22, 34, 46) (never = 0); final_push T(45, 120, 420, 900); limbo 100 at <= 180 s, 0 at >= 900; bite T(0.10, 0.35, 0.70, 0.95); ms_big_damage_share 0 at <= 0.30, 100 at >= 0.60. S3 (w13) = mean of adv_lead_changes per seed T(0, 2, 5, 9); comeback15 T(0.05, 0.20, 0.35, 0.50); even15 T(0.10, 0.30, 0.50, 0.75); decided_to_end per seed 100 at <= 8 min, 0 at >= 20. S4 (w12) = 0.4 x travel (100 at <= 0.30, 0 at >= 0.45) + 0.2 x dead T(0.02, 0.05, 0.13, 0.22) + 0.2 x dead_late (100 at <= 0.15, 0 at >= 0.30) + 0.2 x rta_late (100 at <= 150 s, 0 at >= 330). S5 (w11) = mean of fight_p90_late (0 at <= 6, 100 at >= 14); fight_ramp T(1.0, 1.6, 3.0, 4.5); engaged T(0.25, 0.40, 0.60, 0.75); pk_ramp T(0.8, 1.3, 3.0, 5.0). S6 (w10) = mean of pilot_kill_share (0 at <= 0.40, 100 at >= 0.65); late (0 at <= 0.30, 100 at >= 0.55); involved (0 at <= 0.50, 100 at >= 0.80); unattended (100 at <= 0.10, 0 at >= 0.35). S7 (w9) = mean of skill_rho_small T(0.10, 0.35, 0.65, 0.85); duel_skill_winrate T(0.50, 0.70, 0.85, 0.97); ttk_small_med T(1.5, 4, 10, 20) until re-banded after baseline v2; burst (100 at <= 0.05, 0 at >= 0.25). S8 (w8) = mean of size_parity (100 at <= 0.58, 0 at >= 2.0); arena_small_winrate T(0.15, 0.40, 0.60, 0.85); big_flak_pd_kill_share T(0.05, 0.25, 0.60, 0.85); big_pos_gap (100 at <= 0.5, 0 at >= 1.0); small_late_kill_share T(0.10, 0.30, 0.60, 0.80). S9 (w6) = mean of play_income_share T(0.20, 0.45, 0.70, 0.90); earn_spread T(1.05, 1.3, 2.0, 3.0); pilot_float_30 (100 at <= 1,200, 0 at >= 3,500); treasury_float_late (100 at <= 3,000, 0 at >= 20,000). S10 (w5) = mean of late_spend_ratio (0 at <= 0.30, 100 at >= 0.75); decisions T(0.1, 0.5, 3, 6); asset_kill_share T(0.10, 0.30, 0.70, 0.90). S11 (w4) = mean of assisted T(0.25, 0.50, 0.80, 0.95); low_kp_pilots (100 at <= 1, 0 at >= 4); late_presence (0 at <= 0.30, 100 at >= 0.75). FUN = sum(w_i x S_i)/100, on a 0-100 scale; ingredients 12-13 have weight 0 and act as gates. A sub-metric that cannot be computed (e.g. no big-ship deaths in a seed) is dropped from its ingredient's mean and flagged. The set of scored sub-metrics never depends on optional runs. GATES (pass/fail, outside the score): (1) Halcyon win rate 35-65% over >= 40 seeds (final and baseline only; screens just report it). (2) Mean sim tick within the DESIGN B5 budget at the late-game peak (--profile, 2 seeds). (3) load_all, bot_pilot, support_probe and ui_tour pass, and the PROGRESS arena results hold: 3 fighters beat 3 lances; 2 bombers beat a lone flak frigate; ion beats flak. (4) ms_duel: every scenario keeps its winner, and the upgraded mothership's wins (H, C) end with <= 40% of total HP. (5) With all new variant keys at default, det_hash (900 s normal + 300 s --late) equals main's. (6) Mothership hull, shield and attacker multipliers, the turret backdoor and aggro rules, and turret damage to pilots are unchanged outside a declared variant. (7) Exactly 5 fighters, 4 bombers and 1 corvette per lane wave. (8) Weak-admiral A/B (baseline and merge candidates only): the normal AI admiral wins 60-90% over 20 seeds, sides swapped. (9) Each idea's usage counter is > 0 in the screen. (10) Each idea's requirement_check holds. (11) No NOT-HEADLESS ingredient regresses in the next human playtest. PROTOCOL: (0) MAIN PREP, measurement-only first (det_hash identical): probe v2 with every metric above; Events.damage_applied behind a probe-only flag; tests/lib/adv_index.gd; --skill-jitter (symmetric per seat, separate RNG); --admiral-ai=weak; a symmetric --late start for fun_probe (25:00, fully teched, both sides' inner turrets intact); arena --skill-a/--skill-b and the equal-credit matrix; usage counters in FUN_JSON. Then behaviour fixes, re-baselined: the accidental-assault waypoint fix (mandatory; also reset contact_t/siege_t at assault start); Escort Wing upgrades the single corvette instead of adding an 11th minion (tell the user); data-table prices in AdmiralAI and AdmiralBuilder; the bay_blocker null check; DESIGN A3 synced to the coded respawn (0.9 s/min, cap 50 s). Then shared variant hooks so forks don't collide: an incoming-multiplier list on Mothership and Turret; a per-target minion damage scale in Combat; economy tunables as Rules static getters reading Game.variant; a push-target weight hook in PilotTargeting. (1) BASELINE v2: seeds 101-140 at a 60-min cap with jitter on (about 5 h of machine time), plus the weak-admiral A/B on 20 seeds and the arena metrics. Measure per-seed FUN SD and recompute the seed counts below if it is far from 12. (2) BUDGET: a 60-min-capped match takes 45-53 min of wall time per process on this i5-8500 (6 cores), so one shared job queue runs at most 6 Godot processes, about 8 matches per hour across ALL forks. Each fork gets about 50 match-equivalents. Screens: each idea alone on seeds 101-106 at the 60-min cap, or, for endgame and admiral ideas, 10 symmetric --late runs (20-min cap, about 1/3 of the cost; only S1-from-25:00, S2, S5-late, S6-late and S10 (from 30:00) apply). Order ideas by priority and drop the last one if the budget runs short. Then the fork's best combination on seeds 101-120. Whole programme: about 40 + 300 + 60 = 400 match-equivalents, about 50 machine-hours. (3) SCREEN RULE: keep an idea if its targeted ingredient(s) rise >= 8 points (point estimate), FUN does not fall, every gate passes and its usage counter is > 0; screens reject, they never accept. (4) ACCEPTANCE: a fork's best combination needs FUN +5 with a one-sided 90% paired-bootstrap (1,000 resamples) LB > 0 at n = 20, and no ingredient whose 80% CI lies wholly below -15. The council's merged build vs baseline v2 on seeds 101-140 needs FUN +5 with a 95% LB > 0 and all gates. Report the median, IQR and 95% CI of every metric. Pooled rates need >= 40 seeds (comeback15 SE about 7 points at n = 40). (5) LEVERS AND ALTERNATIVES: hyperspace timing belongs to A2, the turret rebuild lock to D4, siege AI to B1. At most one of C2(b) and F2 merges, and at most one of B3, B5 and F3. No fork reads another fork's variant. Sinks that win (C3, C5, F1) are flagged to the user, because PROGRESS left repeatable sinks as their decision.

## Baseline estimate
Estimated FUN about 52/100 (plausible range 44-60) on the CURRENT build, from the audit's current-build logs (final_1-6 plus seeds 11-16; the c14_* logs predate the per-seat skill fix and are excluded). Baseline v2 will shift it: the waypoint fix, the Escort Wing change and jitter all alter play, so re-measure before forking. S1 match arc about 56: all 12 current-build matches score 56 (9 of 12 ended, finished 29.6-52.2 min; median about 48 counting timeouts as 60); seeds 11-16 alone score 34. S2 endgame arc about 45: onset median about 41 min, scoring about 43; final push about 65 s, about 30; limbo in 2 of 12, about 80; siege bite about 13-17%, about 25, though this number is polluted by the accidental-assault bug's 1-second assaults and is likely higher once they are filtered; big-ship share of mothership damage is unmeasured (likely high, about 80). S3 suspense about 55, low confidence: by kills, 3 of 9 winners were behind at 15:00, but decided stomps take 30+ min to end. S4 downtime about 75 under the 0.30 travel band: travel 0.383 scores 45; dead 0.095 scores 100; dead_late about 0.19 scores 73; rta_late estimated at about 180 s scores 83. S5 ramp about 57 (largest fights 8-14 of 24; estimated late p90 of 9-10; ramp about 1.3; engaged 0.362); the stricter pilot-vs-pilot fight test may lower it. S6 pilot agency about 60 (pilot_kill_share 0.53; after 30:00 about 0.44; involvement about 0.65; unattended turret losses 23/155). S7 skill/TTK about 60, unmeasured: rho guessed at 0.25-0.35, arena duel curve and TTK not yet run. S8 both sizes needed about 60, mostly unmeasured: big hulls out-earn small ones by roughly 1.5-2x per minute once turret and derelict credit are counted (seat-confounded), small ships make perhaps 25% of late pilot kills, and the flak/PD kill share, arena matrix and positioning gap are unknown. S9 economy about 25: play share about 0.30, earn spread probably about 1.1 (little divergence), pilot float at 30:00 2.7k, late treasuries in the tens of thousands. S10 admiral about 55: late spend ratio probably 0.3-0.5, decisions about 0.5-1 per minute from turret rebuilds, asset kills probably about 30%. S11 teamwork about 65, unmeasured. Weakest: economy (25), endgame arc (45), match arc (56), ramp (57). Doc notes: DESIGN A3 says respawn 0.6 s/min capped at 35 s, but rules.gd uses 0.9 s/min capped at 50 s (PROGRESS records the change); and Escort Wing currently makes lane waves 11 strong once researched.

## Fork: pacing_downtime
Cut dead air. Shorten the commute from the hangar to the fight and the late return to action, keep the Homeworld-scale speed hierarchy (heavier is slower), and don't make deaths cheap or the contested big-ship push fast.

Hypotheses:
- H1: Most travel is the commute along friendly lane behind the wave front. A size-scaled slipstream there cuts the travel bucket to <= 0.30 and raises engaged to >= 0.42, while the contested push beyond the front stays at normal speed.

## Fork: combat_roles
Pilots decide fights. Minions stop being the late-game killer, small ships reward flying skill, and big ships reward positioning (escorted means deadly, isolated means dead), so both sizes are needed.

Hypotheses:
- H1: Minions become the top late killer because their damage against pilots scales up to about 2.4x (time scaling x Reinforced Waves) while pilots stay flat. Capping that scaling against pilot ships lifts pilot_kill_share_late to >= 0.55 without slowing turret pushes.
- H2: Big ships out-kill everything because their PD and flak work at full strength wherever the ship is. Making those guns depend on escorts makes isolated big ships die fast and supported ones strong (big_pos_gap <= 0.5), raises big_flak_pd_kill_share in supported fights, and narrows size parity.
- H3: The Lance's tiny pilot-kill rate is mostly the junglers farming camps, but a lone big ship still has no natural predator among strike craft. A rear-zone Lance bonus plus AI that hunts isolated big ships gives small ships a late-game job (small_late_kill_share up).
- H4: A payoff for holding an energy-routing mode rewards timing and raises skill_rho_small and the arena duel curve, provided the AI uses it at every skill level.

### E1 Minions stop out-scaling pilots

When a minion's hit lands on a pilot ship (Unit.K_SHIP), the effective scale becomes min(Minions.scaling(t), 1.15), and TeamState.minion_buff (Reinforced Waves) is ignored. Today stat_scale is passed into the minion's mount updates (minion.gd:134), so Combat divides it out and applies the capped value. Scaling and buffs still apply to minion hull and to damage against minions, turrets, bays and the mothership. MINION_VS_SHIP_MULT stays 0.6.
- Metrics moved: pilot_kill_share and pilot_kill_share_late up; pilot_involved_death_share up. Watch unattended turret losses (should not change) and minutes.

- Effort: S: about 30 lines in combat_scaling.gd (with main's hook).

### E2 Escorts make big ships deadly

### E4 A payoff for holding an energy-routing mode (no new skill gate)

Risks: Weaker anti-pilot minions plus escort-dependent big ships may slow pushes and lengthen matches; pair with fork 2. Overcharge can make small-ship fights bursty. The AI may not exploit escorts or rear attacks the way a human would, which could understate or misstate their value.

## Fork: admiral_command
The admiral makes frequent, legible, high-impact decisions all match, especially after the tech tree is done, and those decisions create objectives for pilots. The AI admiral plays competently and reacts to the enemy fleet, while a weak admiral never leaves the team helpless.

Hypotheses:
- H1: Admiral decisions dry up after 31-35 min and the treasury piles up. Command assets with costs and cooldowns give 0.5-3 decisions per minute late and push late_spend_ratio to >= 0.75.
- H2: Treasury-funded bounty orders on targets align AI pilots, and give humans a reason to follow the admiral, so turrets die with pilots present and late fights grow.
- H3: A one-button Fortify gives defenders a skill-based answer to sieges without causing stalls, so final_push lands in 2-7 min.
- H4: An AI admiral that reacts to the enemy fleet (counter-tech and fleet-composition calls) adds counter-play while the weak-admiral A/B stays within 60-90%.

### F1 Command assets: Sensor Sweep and Supply Drop (Barrage as an optional value)
- Mechanic: Variant assets with values on and full. New AdmiralCommand orders with a treasury cost and a per-team cooldown, targeting a point in friendly vision. Sensor Sweep (250 credits, 60 s): a temporary vision source of radius 3,500 at the point for 8 s that respects asteroid occlusion like any other vision (it reveals what the lanes and gaps let it see, not through rocks). Supply Drop (400 credits, 90 s): 4 repair drones (the existing destroyable type, bounty 10) fly from the mothership at 900 u/s and heal friendly pilot ships within 1,200 of the point for 8 s at bay-drone rates. Value full adds Mothership Barrage (600 credits, 150 s; point within 12,000 of the mothership): a red circle shown to BOTH teams for 3 s, then 600 shell damage in a 500 radius (the damage table applies; kills are credited to the team as 'admiral'). It is screened last. A new AdmiralAssets part drives the AI: sweep when a siege or defence is active, or when a lane has a turret under attack and no enemy visible; supply the largest friendly fight cluster whose average hull is below 60%; barrage when 3 or more enemy ships are within 500 of a point in range. Usage counter: assets used by type.
- Metrics moved: admiral decisions and late_spend_ratio up; treasury_float_late down; asset_kill_share up; fight outcomes and comeback windows. Watch pilot_kill_share (barrage kills count as non-pilot) and TTK.
- Requirement check: Adds admiral powers only. Wards are unchanged, repair drones can still be destroyed, and 'players cannot see through asteroids' holds because the sweep is occluded. The mothership's 'decent point defense, weak to large ships' profile is unchanged. Keeps.

## Rejected ideas
- A surrender vote or mercy rule (LoL 4-1 vote, Smite): violates 'only destroying the mothership should end the match'.
- A timed epic neutral at the crossing (a Baron/Roshan-style Derelict Dreadnought with a team buff), and the late 'Elder Core' execute buff that depends on it: the brief's dynamic events were 'moving debris, temporary sensor blackout, or a high-value/spawnable derelict', and the user said 'Remove the dynamic event zones'. Replaced by D2, a converted permanent camp with a personal reward.
- Salvage beacons or capture points with a 'Sensor Blackout' reward (HotS Cursed Hollow): temporary sensor-blackout zones are one of the removed dynamic events, and the second prompt removed capture points (drones replaced capture).
- Adding an 11th super or siege minion after a breach (LoL inhibitors): breaks the required wave of 10. For the same reason, the existing Escort Wing tech's extra corvette is changed in main to upgrade the single corvette (to be confirmed with the user).
- Breach corvettes with full damage against the mothership (the original B2): minions, not large ships, would close games; replaced by a breach tank.
- Lowering mothership hull or shields, or raising the big-ship multiplier, to shorten matches: breaks 'a lot of health' and the ms_duel 'barely' tuning; now a gate.
- A shared AdvIndex helper in scripts/core that game mechanics read: Goodhart; the index stays in tests/lib, and mechanics define their own scoreboard-visible triggers.
- HotS-style shared team XP, or a flat catch-up income trickle for the trailing team (including an equal 12-way split of comeback bounties): passive and unearned, and it suppresses the individual skill reward the user asked for.
- Moving the mothership before 20:00, or letting an undocked admiral move it: violates 'can be moved by a docked admiral ... starting at 20 minutes'.
- Automatic respawn at a forward repair bay or waypoint that skips the mothership: removes the hangar visit where kits must be bought and dilutes respawn strain. A2 uses a hyperspace launch from the dock instead.
- Selling repair kits or gear at repair bays: the requirements say kits are bought at the mothership; module swaps at bays already exist.
- Pilot mining of hardpoints, or personal extractors, as a late money source or sink: explicitly removed by the user.
- Making the mothership weaker after big treasury spends: explicitly reversed by the user.
- Raising the global respawn curve toward LoL/Dota late values (60-100 s): the late dead share is already 16-25%, and travel is the real downtime. Only A4's size-scaled variant is tested.
- A home-field fog advantage for defenders (Dota high ground): makes sieges harder when they are already the weakest link, deepening stalemates.
- A push meter that strengthens the next waves after kills (Star Wars Squadrons morale): makes minions stronger, the opposite of the fix for minion dominance.
- An early 'Feats of Strength' race: the early game already engages fast (first blood at 1.7-1.9 min); it adds an early snowball without fixing any late-game problem.
- Letting big ships enter the jungle, or widening the zoom for readability: violates 'only small ships can travel through' and the max-zoom lock.
- A commander mutiny/eject vote or swapping the admiral mid-match: the role choice is locked by requirement, and there is only one human.
- Removing or capping free minion waves late: free waves are a requirement. E1 caps minion scaling against pilots instead.
- A fourth jungle clearing per quadrant (the original D2): a map-builder change with reachability risk; an existing camp is converted instead.
- A 60-degree rear blind arc for capitals (the original E3): it already exists in the mount arcs.
- A Sensor Sweep that sees through asteroids (the original F1): conflicts with 'players cannot see through asteroids'; the sweep is occluded.
- A separate 40-seed --skill-jitter batch for every variant: jitter is on in every FUN batch instead.
- A passive-admiral A/B as a scored metric: with no turrets the passive side loses almost every match, whatever the fork; replaced by a weak-admiral gate.
- Logistic-regression reweighting of the advantage index on 40 matches: unstable; replaced by pre-registered weights plus an AUC check.
- AI routing or charge behaviour gated on a new skill threshold (the original E4): it raises skill_rho by construction.

## Critic: problems fixed
- [Protocol: compute budget] The plan needs roughly 1,100 full 60-minute matches: a 40-seed baseline, a separate 40-seed jitter batch, a 40-seed A/B, 26 ideas x 20 screening seeds, and 6 fork combinations x 40 seeds plus their own jitter batches. On this machine one 60-min-capped match takes 45-53 min of wall time per process (scratchpad fun_base logs from 2026-10-14: seed_12 ran 7:57 to 8:50 with 7 processes in parallel; seed_16 took 47 min with 2). The CPU is a 6-core i5-8500, so all forks together get about 7-8 matches per hour. That is about 150 machine-hours, and forks running at the same time compete for the same 6 cores. -> FIX: Set an explicit budget of about 400 match-equivalents (about 50 machine-hours), with one shared job queue capped at 6 Godot processes. Turn skill jitter on in every FUN batch so there is no separate rho batch. Screen each idea on 6 seeds (101-106), or on 10 cheap symmetric --late runs for endgame and admiral ideas (start at 25:00, 20-min cap, about 1/3 of the cost). Run each fork's best combination on 20 seeds. Only baseline v2 and the merged build get 40 seeds.
- [Protocol: statistics] A rule change makes the RNG diverge within seconds, so 'paired' seeds share little beyond the map and give almost no variance reduction. If per-seed FUN SD is 10-15, the 95% CI half-width of a difference at n=40 is about 5 points. A true +5 effect would then pass 'LB > 0' only about half the time. The 'no ingredient down by more than 15' check will fire on noise at n=20. Most single ideas move FUN by only 1-3 points, so none can clear a +5 bar alone. -> FIX: Measure per-seed SD on baseline v2 and size n from it. Run the ingredient-drop check on the 80% CI, not on point estimates.

With 3-4 alive they can grind a mothership down with no pilot present. - [Metric: travel (S4a)] The travel bucket means 'alive, not engaged, no enemy within 2,500'. It lumps the commute together with idle station-holding behind a cleared wave, and slipstream and recall only cut the commute. A 0.25 target also runs against the brief's Homeworld-scale travel times (trips of 20-60+ s). -> FIX: Split the bucket into commute (speed >= 40% of f_max) and idle, report both, and give full marks to the combined bucket at 0.30 or below.
- [Metric: fight detection (S5, S11c)] A cluster counts as a fight if any member's last_damage_time is within 4 s, and any hit sets that: minions, turrets or rocks. So 3v3 pilots standing in a minion clash count as a team fight with no pilot-vs-pilot exchange, and AI clumping (D1) would score without anyone fighting. -> FIX: Require a pilot-sourced hit between the two teams inside the cluster within 4 s, taken from the new damage hook or from damage_log entries that carry an enemy profile.
- [Measurability: per-hit data] TTK chains, mothership damage by attacker size, and pilot-vs-pilot fight checks are all approximated by polling last_damage_time every 0.25-1 s. There is no per-hit event: Combat.apply_damage emits nothing. -> FIX: Add a measurement-only Events.damage_applied(target, info, amount, hull_dmg) in Combat.apply_damage, behind a world flag that only the probe sets. det_hash must stay identical.
- [S3 calibration] A logistic model with 4-5 weights fitted on 40 matches has huge coefficient intervals, so 'use the fitted weights if they differ by more than 2x' will flip on noise. The project's tooling (GDScript, PowerShell) also has no stats library. -> FIX: Pre-register the weights and the |A| >= 1 threshold. After the baseline, report the AUC of sign(A) predicting the winner at 15, 20 and 25 min. Only if AUC at 20 min is below 0.65, switch once to a pre-specified alternative (drop FV, double K5), then freeze it.
- [S10 admiral] A fully passive admiral (no turrets, bays or wards) will lose nearly every match, so the 65-80% A/B band scores about 0 for every fork. S10 also averages either 2 or 3 sub-metrics depending on whether the A/B ran, so FUN is not comparable between screens and merges. Decisions per minute counts drone builds and AI churn, so AI purchase spam can inflate it. -> FIX: Turn the A/B into a gate against a weak admiral (plan steps 90 s apart, turrets at inner nodes only, no bays, wards, sieges or moves); the normal admiral should win 60-90%. Score S10 on late_spend_ratio, filtered decisions per minute, and asset_kill_share.
- [S9 economy] play_income_share is essentially a ratio of the constants C1 changes: cutting passive income raises it whether or not play decides income. -> FIX: Add earn_spread (p75/p25 of pilots' earned) and report corr(skill, earned) from the jittered batch.

B3, B5 and F3 are three versions of a defender window. A2 and B1 both change siege-time AI. These overlaps blur what the council is comparing and collide in the same files. -> FIX: Assign lever owners: hyperspace timing = A2, rebuild lock = D4, siege AI = B1. Mark alternatives, of which at most one merges: C2(b) vs F2, and B3 vs B5 vs F3. No fork reads another fork's variant.

The asteroid layout and jungle reachability would change for the whole variant: a risky map-generation change for a single idea. -> FIX: Under the variant, convert the existing carrier camp nearest the connector in each quadrant into the Derelict Battleship. Sensor Sweep 'through asteroids' runs against 'players cannot see through asteroids': Scout Ping is a P1-era exception, not a precedent for an admiral-wide power. -> FIX: Trim F1 to Sensor Sweep (a temporary vision source at the point that respects asteroid occlusion) plus Supply Drop. A wider defend trigger repeats the 14,000 radius that kept both teams 'defending' all match and blocked every siege. -> FIX: Keep both as variant values tested against current behaviour, and cite this history. B1(d) fires only for 2 or more seen enemy big ships that are closing (velocity toward our hull above 40 u/s), and never cancels our own assault.
- [AI usage] The rule 'the AI must use it, or the sim shows nothing' is not enforced. C2 has no AI use at all, and no idea logs whether the AI actually used the mechanic. -> FIX: Each variant logs a usage counter in FUN_JSON (redeploys, recalls, buybacks, Mk II purchases, bounty-structure kills, charges spent, assets used). ## Critic: missing

- No metric for small ships staying relevant late ('both should be needed to push lanes'). Added small_late_kill_share.
