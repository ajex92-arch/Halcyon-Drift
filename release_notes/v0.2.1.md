## What is new in v0.2.1

**Pilots can look around the whole map.**
- Move the mouse to a screen edge, drag with the middle mouse button, or click the minimap. How far out you can zoom is unchanged.
- Press Space to put the camera on your ship (on your mothership when you have no ship). Hold Space to keep it centered while you fly; let go and the camera is free again.
- Your camera jumps to your ship when it launches.
- Space no longer fires: shoot with the left mouse button.
- Losing focus (Alt+Tab or the Windows key) now ends a middle-button drag and lets go of a held fire button, and the camera edge-pans only while the pointer is inside the game window.

**The computer pilots open the match differently.**
- They wait by their mothership for 10 seconds, then go out to the three middle points (the Crossings) and hold them until their first minion wave arrives. Before, they drifted out with the wave.
- A pilot who missed the first wave does not fly out for a second opening. Junglers that are losing a fight in the opening fall back, like the lane pilots.
- As Admiral, a rally flag you place in the first minutes now pulls seats 4, 5, 6 and the flex pilot off their Crossings; the computer Admiral's own flag does not.
- The computer Admiral builds its Crossing turret before extra turrets at home, and its first one where its own ships are, instead of always trying the Crossings in the same order.

**What to try:** tap and hold Space in a fight, and watch the first two minutes of a match.

**Known issues**
- The early game is bloodier than in v0.2.0: the pilots now meet at the Crossings in the first minute, and most of them fly home to repair soon after, so the Crossings thin out early.
- Ships still sometimes get stuck in the rocks. A fix is being worked on.
- When one side takes all three Crossings early, the other side may build no Crossing turret for most of the match.
- These findings come from matches between computer players.
