## What is new in v0.3.0

**Play either team.**
- The start screen has a new TEAM row: Aurelian (amber, on the left of the map) or Vhorr (red, on the right). It works for both seats, Pilot and Admiral, and the other team is all computer. The map is not mirrored: as Vhorr your camera and your mothership are on the right. Your pick is remembered.
- The menus' highlight colour follows your team: amber for Aurelian, red for Vhorr.

**One set of rules for everyone.** The RULE SET row is gone. Every match now plays the rules that were called Council's pick in v0.2.0: late minions no longer out-fight pilots, the Lance hunts lone big ships, the computer Admiral spends a big treasury, gear has Mk II upgrades, a team may have up to 60 bought minions, and a turret node stays locked longer after its turret falls.

**Difficulty levels.** A new DIFFICULTY row: Easy, Medium, Hard, Very hard and Extreme. In a match where you play against the computer, the enemy team earns 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5 or 2.0 times as much. Your own team's income is never changed. Medium is the default and plays as before; your pick is remembered.

**New looks.**
- Aurelian ships are steel, with armour plates, glass canopies, amber stripes and engine lights. Vhorr ships have an alien look (dark, bladed hulls with glowing veins), and their engine flames are a quarter smaller than Aurelian's.
- Every gun that turns on a pilot's ship, a lane tower, a Repair Bay or the Aurelian mothership is drawn as a small turret, turned the way the gun points.
- Minions are plain, solid shapes that cannot be mistaken for a pilot's ship, different for each team.

**Smaller minion waves, with new names and heavier handling.**
- The top and bottom lanes get 3 Snub-Fighters, 1 Light Bomber and 1 Patrol Leader; the middle lane gets 5 Snub-Fighters, 4 Light Bombers and 2 Patrol Leaders (21 a team, down from 30). The old names were Fighter, Bomber and Escort Corvette.
- Minions are slower to change speed (10%) and direction (20%), and their noses turn 20% slower. Top speed is the same.

**Fairer money for helpers.**
- When an enemy minion falls, the pilot who made the last hit gets 75% of the credits and the pilots who did at least a tenth of the damage in the last 15 seconds share the other 25%.
- When a pilot's ship falls, helpers share a third of its bounty and the killer keeps the rest. The helpers' share comes out of the reward, never on top of it.

**Computer pilots go for last hits.** A lane pilot with nothing to shoot no longer parks behind its wave. It flies up to the enemy minions fighting near the front of its wave and goes for last hits. Pilots also no longer wait at stations that sit right on top of their own turrets.

**Bigger ships, easier targets, turrets without shields.**
- The Destroyer and the Battlecruiser are 1.5 times as big, the Flak Frigate and the Ion Frigate 1.25 times, in how they look, how they are hit and how they collide.
- The Scout, Fighter, Lance Fighter and Bomber are 10% easier to hit. Their bodies are unchanged.
- Turrets have no shields, and nothing repairs a turret.

**Screens and looks.**
- The picture stretches with the window (smallest window 1280x720), the big panels stay clear of the ship readout and the key line, and F11 or Alt+Enter switches between the window and borderless fullscreen (in a browser, Alt+Enter).
- The drift arrow shows on your ship only, a hit flashes a ship's outline and leaves its fill alone, and the lane and node names on the map are never taller than about 60 pixels.

**What to try:** play a match as Vhorr, try a harder level, and watch a wave meet at the Crossing.

**Known issues**
- The big Aurelian ships have several engine nozzles but one exhaust trail until a later release.
- Ships still get stuck in the rocks now and then, the big Battlecruisers most of all; the fix is not in this release.
