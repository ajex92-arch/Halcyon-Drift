## What is new in v0.3.1

**The HUD is back in place.** In v0.3.0 the browser version and the Windows download drew the top bar, your name and credits, the gear slots, the key line, the hints and the big messages on top of one another in the top-left corner. Everything is where it belongs again, at every window size.

Nothing else changes from v0.3.0: the rules, the looks and the controls are the same.

**Known issues**
- The big Aurelian ships have several engine nozzles but one exhaust trail until a later release.
- Ships still get stuck in the rocks now and then, the big Battlecruisers most of all; the fix is not in this release.
