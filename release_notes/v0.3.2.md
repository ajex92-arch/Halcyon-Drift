## What is new in v0.3.2

**Ships get stuck in the rocks far less often.** A computer pilot that is stuck now backs out along the track it came in on, routes start at points the ship can really fly to, small ships no longer fly at a target through a rock wall, and ships slow down when they leave the jungle through a gate. In 12 test matches the times a ship got stuck on rocks fell from 141 to 15. Matches play differently from v0.3.1 from the first minute.

**A pause menu.** Press **P** or **Esc** during a match: the game pauses and a menu opens with Resume, How to play, Restart, Quit to start, Full screen and Quit game (the browser version has no Quit game). **P** again resumes.

**In the browser, a click lands where the pointer is from the first load,** also on pages less than 720 pixels high.

**Small fixes** in the rule sets and the launch options. None of them changes the standard rules.

**Known issues**
- A minion already flying when its team finishes a Reinforced Waves research hits pilot ships a little weaker than the rules say; the fix is being tried in the test build first.
- The big Aurelian ships have several engine nozzles but one exhaust trail until a later release.
- Ships still get stuck now and then, though far less often in the rocks; the big ships still stop now and then in the open lanes while they turn.
